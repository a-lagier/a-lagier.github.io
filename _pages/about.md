I am a Master's student in Computer Science at [École Normale Supérieure de Lyon](https://www.ens-lyon.fr/). My research interests are **image reconstruction**, **diffusion and flow-based generative models**, and **neural architectures**.

I am currently a research intern at [MaLGa](https://malga.unige.it/) in Genoa, supervised by Luca Calatroni, where I apply generative methods to inverse problems in microscopy. Before that, I worked on provably convergent generative solvers for inverse problems in imaging at Inria Lyon with Ségolène Martin, Mathurin Massias and Anne Gagneux.
